Welcome to the Bodega

The Bodega is a beloved local establishment that has been serving the community for decades. A small, family-owned business offering groceries, snacks, beverages, and household essentials — known for its friendly staff, convenient location, and affordable prices.

The Bodega's History — Lens of the Neighbourhood

Founded in the early 1970s, the Bodega has a rich history deeply intertwined with the neighbourhood it serves. Over the decades it has evolved and adapted while staying true to its roots: quality products, excellent service, and a genuine sense of community. More than a store, it's a gathering place where neighbours connect, share stories, and support one another.
